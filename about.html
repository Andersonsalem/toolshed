How this site works

The short version: the tools are free, your data stays on your device, and I'll tell you plainly what each tool can and can't do.

Who makes this

I'm one person building these tools while I look for a job. They're tools I wanted for myself, so I made them properly and put them online for anyone to use.

It's free, and it stays free

  • Every feature of every tool works for everyone.
  • No accounts, no sign-ups, no email collection.
  • No ads and no sponsored placements.

What happens to your data

  • It stays in your browser. The tools run on your device. Anything you type or open is processed there and isn't sent to me or anyone else.
  • A few tools need outside data, and say so. For example, the Weather Planner asks a free weather service (Open-Meteo) for forecasts. These tools are marked “Uses the internet” on the home page, say on their own page what they send, and send only what's needed: the place you ask about, never your name or anything you've saved. Each tool limits how often it asks, and keeps answers for a while so it asks less.
  • Saved data lives in your browser's storage. Some tools remember your work between visits using your browser's local storage. It's only on this device and in this browser.
  • Clearing your browser data deletes it. So does using a private window. Tools that save data have an Export button so you can keep a backup file.
  • No tracking. No analytics, no cookies, no fonts or scripts loaded from other companies. The pages and their code all come from this site.
  • The web host sees normal traffic. Like any website, the server that delivers these pages may keep standard logs (IP address, which page was loaded). I don't add anything to that.

Limits, honestly

  • Calculators give estimates, not financial, tax or legal advice. Each tool says what it assumes.
  • Tools are provided as-is. I test them, but bugs happen. Keep backups of anything important.
  • Tools marked Planned aren't built yet.

Supporting the site

If a tool saved you time or money and you'd like to say thanks, you can support me here. It's entirely optional. Supporters don't get extra features, and nobody is treated differently.

There's no way to pay yet. When there is, it'll be optional, it won't unlock anything, and nobody will be treated differently for not paying.
