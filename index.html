Toolshed

Free, all of it

Every feature works for everyone. Nothing is locked, there's no trial, and there's no "pro" version.

Private by design

Most tools run entirely on your device: what you type or open isn't uploaded. The few that need outside data, like weather, say so and send only what's needed. No analytics, no cookies, no third-party scripts.

Built by a job seeker

I'm building these while I look for work. If one helps you, you can chip in. It's optional and gets you nothing extra. There's no way to pay yet, and you'll never need to.
