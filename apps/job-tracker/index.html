How these numbers are counted
  • Applied: applications you've sent. Ones marked Saved aren't counted.
  • Reply rate: share of those that got any answer: an interview, an offer or a rejection. No response isn't a reply.
  • Interviews and Offers: applications that reached that stage at any point, even if they later closed. An offer also counts as an interview.
  • Numbers only include what you've entered here.
