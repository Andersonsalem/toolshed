Good to know

  • Line by line suits code, lists and data: each line is matched with its counterpart, and changed words inside a line are highlighted. Word by word suits writing, where a sentence edit can reflow a whole paragraph.
  • Ignoring capitals or extra spaces hides those differences from the view. The downloaded .patch file always includes every difference.
  • Long stretches of unchanged lines are folded away; click them to show them.
  • Plain-text files only (.txt, .md, .csv, .json, code…). For Word or PDF documents, copy the text out and paste it here.
  • Nothing you paste or open is saved or sent anywhere. Only the settings above are remembered in this browser.
