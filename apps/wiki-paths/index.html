Wikipedia Paths

Pick two topics and find a chain of Wikipedia links from one to the other, then read about the pages you'd pass through on the way.

Uses the internet This tool reads Wikipedia's public links and summaries.

It sends only the topics you search for. A search makes at most about 45 small requests, one at a time, and stops there: it doesn't hammer Wikipedia, so it may not find every route.

Article text and pictures come from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons; text is available under CC BY-SA 4.0. Each card links to its article and its full credits.
