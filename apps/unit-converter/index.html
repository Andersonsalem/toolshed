    Good to know

    • Conversions use the official definitions: an inch is exactly 2.54 cm, a pound exactly 0.45359237 kg, a US gallon exactly 3.785411784 liters. Each result says whether the factor is exact or rounded.
    • US and UK units differ. A UK pint is about 20% bigger than a US pint, and so are UK gallons. Recipes from Australia, Canada and New Zealand usually mean the 250 mL metric cup.
    • Cooking conversions between cups and grams depend on the ingredient (a cup of flour weighs much less than a cup of sugar), so they aren't included rather than guessed.
    • Currencies aren't included: their rates change by the minute and need a live data source.
    • Results show up to 10 significant digits. Typing is calculated in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
