How this is calculated
  • Every amount is turned into a monthly figure: weekly × 52 ÷ 12, every two weeks × 26 ÷ 12, yearly ÷ 12.
  • Spending includes the minimum payments on your debts.
  • Runway: your savings plus one-off money, shrinking each month by spending minus the income that keeps coming. No interest on savings, no price changes, no taxes on one-off money.
  • Debts: interest is charged monthly (yearly rate ÷ 12), then payments are made. Minimums are paid on every debt; extra money goes to one debt at a time, and a cleared debt's payment rolls on to the next. Fees, rate changes and new borrowing aren't included.
  • Goals: what's left to save, split evenly over the months until the date. No interest is assumed.
  • From statements: the total spent in each category, divided by the number of months the statements cover.
