Fitness Log

Plan and track your workouts, and keep a simple food diary. No account, no ads, and nothing leaves your device.

This is a diary, not medical advice. It doesn't set calorie targets for you: everyone's needs differ, so if you want a personal target, ask a doctor or registered dietitian. If tracking food starts to feel stressful, it's fine to stop.
