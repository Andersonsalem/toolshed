Offer Comparator

Put job offers side by side: total pay, pay after tax, what's left after living costs, and pay per hour of your time.

You bring the numbers. Tax rules and living costs differ too much from place to place for this page to guess them honestly, so you enter your own estimates. Results are only as good as what you put in, and this isn't financial or tax advice. Nothing you enter is uploaded.

Your browser is blocking storage (this often happens in private windows), so your offers won't be saved when you close this page.

Enter every offer in the same currency. Amounts can be typed like 95000, 95,000 or 95k.

    Where the pay comes from
      How this is calculated
      • All figures are per year. With more than one year selected, they're the average per year over that time.
      • No raises are assumed. The bonus is counted at the amount you expect, which usually isn't guaranteed.
      • The signing bonus is spread over the years being compared, so it matters most in the first year. Repayment terms if you leave early aren't considered.
      • Equity is counted at the value you entered and vests evenly over its vesting period. Cliffs, refreshers and changes in value aren't modelled. Private company equity can end up worth nothing.
      • Tax is your rate applied to salary, bonus, signing bonus and equity. Retirement contributions and other benefits are counted as untaxed.
      • Pay per hour uses total pay before tax, divided by the hours you work and commute, after paid days off.
