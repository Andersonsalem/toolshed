    Keyword match

    How this is calculated
    • Key terms are tools and skills from a built-in list (like "Python" or "project management", in any common spelling) and from your own skills list, plus other words and two-word phrases the job description uses at least twice.
    • Everyday words and job-ad boilerplate, like "team" or "experience", are skipped.
    • Terms the job description mentions more often count more. The score is the weighted share your resume contains.
    • Word endings are matched loosely: "managing" matches "managed".
    • Some terms have built-in near-synonyms ("developer" for "engineer", "led" for "leadership"). If your resume uses one, the term is listed under Said differently but doesn't add to the score, because hiring filters usually look for exact words.
    • Other synonyms aren't recognised, so a term can show as missing even if you describe the same thing in other words.
    • There's no "passing" score. A higher number means more overlap in wording, nothing more.
